### Who this Guide is for

This assembly guide is for our **MK2 AltMill**. It includes updated wiring harnesses and bump stops for machines shipped July 2025 and newer. If your machine shipped with stainless steel dust shields, please refer to the [MK1 assembly process](https://resources.sienci.com/view/am-best-practices/).

### Tips

Before you dive into the assembly of your machine, we have a few recommendations to make your experience a good one:

- Due to the weight of the components, it's best to assemble the AltMill with another person.
- The expected assembly time is **4–6 hours**, so make sure to allow ample time and pace yourself to avoid errors, damage or injury during the process.
- Use the appropriate tool for each fastener. You may use a drill or driver for quicker assembly where permitted by the instructions, but be careful not to overtighten screws.
- Before tightening fasteners, take note of whether they should be firmly or loosely secured. Some steps require screws to be left loose initially so that components can be aligned later.
- Keep the AltMill packaging, as some of it will be used during assembly.
- Ensure at least **6’ × 12’ of clear working space** to set up the machine’s table/base.
- Keep hardware in its respective bag until needed so you can use the labels to find the correct type.
- If you are uncomfortable with using software, we highly recommend having a **computer-savvy** friend or family member help you with the final few steps, starting with the [First Movements](https://resources.sienci.com/view/am-mk2-first-movements/) article.
